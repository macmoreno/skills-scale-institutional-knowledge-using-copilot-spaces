# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository centralizes OctoAcme’s project management knowledge so team members can find the processes, roles, and practices used across the organization in one place.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured project lifecycle that begins with initiation and ends with retrospective and continuous improvement. New work is evaluated for business need, stakeholder alignment, and measurable success before the team moves into planning. Once an initiative is approved, the team translates it into a prioritized backlog, milestones, dependencies, and a release plan. During execution, work is tracked through standups, reviews, and ongoing risk monitoring to keep progress visible and decisions timely.

The organization also defines clear accountability across roles. Product leads define the desired outcomes and priority; project managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and stakeholder communication; developers build and test solutions; QA validates acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input, approvals, and business context. This role clarity supports better collaboration and reduces uncertainty about who is responsible for decisions and follow-through.

Communication is a foundational element of OctoAcme’s process model. Teams use regular standups, weekly status reviews, milestone check-ins, and stakeholder updates to maintain alignment and surface blockers early. Risks and dependencies are captured in a risk register and escalated through defined paths when they require broader coordination. By keeping a single source of truth for status and decisions, OctoAcme reduces confusion and improves response time when issues arise.

Quality and release discipline are built into project delivery rather than treated as a final checkpoint. Teams define acceptance criteria, follow consistent pull request practices, and validate work through testing, CI checks, security scans, and manual QA where appropriate. Releases require verification against readiness criteria, rollback planning, and post-deployment checks so that the organization can move quickly without sacrificing stability.

## Project management process summary

- Initiation: validate the problem, confirm stakeholders, define success metrics, and decide whether work should proceed.
- Planning: create the backlog, assign roles, estimate work, identify dependencies, and establish the release timeline.
- Execution and tracking: run the plan with visible progress, regular reviews, and escalation of blockers when needed.
- Risk and communication: document and monitor risks, align stakeholders, and keep decision-making transparent.
- Release and deployment: verify readiness, deploy with safeguards, and confirm that the change is working in production.
- Retrospective and improvement: capture learnings, assign action items, and iterate on the process over time.

## Purpose

The goal of these process documents is to make OctoAcme’s project management knowledge easier to find, share, and apply consistently across the organization. By standardizing the framework while remaining flexible in execution, the team can improve onboarding, reduce single-person dependency, and support more reliable project outcomes.
